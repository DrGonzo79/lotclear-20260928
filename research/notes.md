# Source notes

## Public concept

- Source: [Handle vehicle titles for independent car dealers](https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/handle-vehicle-titles-for-independent-car-dealers-331857f4)
- Source date: 2026-09-28
- Idea: a title service for small used-car lots that checks paperwork against state rules, flags missing items, files it and follows rejections until title clearance.
- Pain described: a packet can be rejected weeks after sale for a signature, form or state-rule mismatch; funding and trade-in resale remain blocked.
- Suggested offer: $49, $79 or $99 per title, possibly starting free to earn trust.
- Timing signal: states are digitizing titles and some are newly allowing outside-company access. The newsletter links to an example state modernization program; this was not independently validated in the prototype.

## Access note

The public detail URL returned a Vercel security checkpoint to lightweight fetch and redirected the OpenClaw browser to login. Scores, keyword data, offer ladder, detailed growth signals, execution difficulty, revenue potential and GTM fields were unavailable and are intentionally not invented.

## Product interpretation

The sharp wedge is a cited, human-supervised packet preflight, not an autonomous “AI title service.” LotClear demonstrates a transparent state rules check using fictional data and explicitly avoids filing, legal advice and claims of current legal completeness.
